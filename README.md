# Hospital Management System – Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes hospital data to provide insights into **patients, doctors, departments, medical stock, revenue, costs, and overall hospital performance**.

The main goal is to transform raw hospital data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, analysis, and an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the number of patients and doctors across departments.
- Understand department-level performance.
- Analyze hospital revenue.
- Analyze medical stock, costs, and profits.
- Identify potential financial losses from expired medicines.
- Analyze patient status and demographics.
- Evaluate doctors' availability and salaries.
- Calculate the average patient length of stay.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for hospital management.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- **Power Query**
- **Pivot Tables**
- **Pivot Charts**
- **DAX / Measures**
- **Excel Dashboard**

## 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of the hospital through several key sections:

### Departments

- Number of departments
- Patients per department
- Doctors per department
- Department revenue

### Medicine & Medical Stock

- Medical stock analysis
- Medicine profit
- Potential financial loss from expired medicines
- Test revenue

### Doctors & Patients

- Doctor availability
- Patient status
- Patient gender
- Average length of stay
- Total doctors' salary

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes important KPIs such as:

- **Total Departments**
- **Total Patients**
- **Total Doctors**
- **Total Revenue**
- **Total Medicine Profit**
- **Tests Revenue**
- **Average Length of Stay**
- **Total Doctors' Salary**

## 🔍 Business Insights

The analysis helps hospital management understand:

- Which departments have the highest patient volume.
- Which departments generate the highest revenue.
- How medical stock contributes to profitability.
- The potential financial impact of expired medicines.
- Doctor availability across departments.
- Patient distribution and status.
- Hospital staffing and salary costs.
- Patient stay duration and its potential operational impact.

## 💡 Business Value

This analysis can support hospital management in:

- Improving resource allocation.
- Optimizing staffing decisions.
- Monitoring department performance.
- Reducing potential losses from expired medical stock.
- Understanding revenue and profitability.
- Improving inventory management.
- Making data-driven operational decisions.
